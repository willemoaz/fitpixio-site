Questions

Will my photos be uploaded anywhere?

No. FitPixio processes everything on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics on your photos.

Does it reduce quality?

It finds the highest quality that fits your limit. If the target allows it, quality stays at maximum — the app only compresses as much as necessary.

Can I convert PNG to JPG and back?

Yes — JPG, PNG and WebP work in every direction. Note that PNG is lossless, so tiny exact sizes are sometimes only possible as JPG; the app will tell you and offer one-tap retry.

Does it work with iPhone HEIC photos?

Yes. HEIC photos open directly and convert to the format your upload form wants.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Pro is tied to your Play Store / App Store account. Reinstall with the same account and tap “Restore” — no code needed.
