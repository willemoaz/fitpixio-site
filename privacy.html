Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Your photos never leave your device. FitPixio processes everything on your phone. We have no accounts, no servers storing your images, and no analytics on your photos.

Photos and files

When you choose a photo, it is read, resized, compressed and verified locally on your device. The original file is never modified and no image data is transmitted anywhere. Processed copies are stored only in the app's temporary storage and your gallery if you choose to save them.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data and require no account. The app stores two counters on your device only: remaining free daily conversions and your Pro unlock status.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. The paid Pro version contains no ads and no ad SDK data collection. Users in the EU/EEA are asked for consent before any personalized advertising.

In-app purchases

Pro upgrades are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment information; the stores' privacy policies apply to those transactions.

Permissions

  • Photos / media access — to let you pick a picture to convert.
  • Camera (optional) — to take a picture to convert.
  • Gallery save — only when you tap Save, to store the result.

Children

FitPixio is a general-utility app with no user-generated content sharing and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new revision date.
